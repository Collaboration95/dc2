## Presentation guidance (pp. 1–2)

- Emphasise significant achievements and distinctive design choices rather than listing ordinary features.
- Use a top down narrative: requirements and high level decisions before details.
- Keep slides few and readable from the room; do not read slide text aloud.
- Rehearse the timed demo and quality attribute evidence. The demo is expected to show behavior, not only screenshots of architecture.

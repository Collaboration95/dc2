Which documents govern the presentation?

The current GC2 briefing, submitted Team 17 proposal and presentation guide govern the showcase. The older SWE5006 ZIP contributes an engineering completeness checklist; its dates, marking weights, examples and demo timing do not replace GC2 guidance.

Both local presentation guides place Team 17 on 26 October, 14:35–15:15 SGT. Their copies are linked in the showcase chapter; check Canvas for any later change. No completed final GC2 report was found in the inspected repository and local course folders. The report template is a template, not a finished submission.

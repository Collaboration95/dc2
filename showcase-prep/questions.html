Data & recovery · Level 1

What is the difference between retrying a job and receiving a duplicate message?
A retry creates a new attempt for accepted work. A duplicate message repeats the same transport instruction and must not create a second logical operation.
Review the explanation →

Data & recovery · Level 1

Why pin a VersionId when the object already has a key?
A key names a location whose contents can change; the version pin selects the exact accepted bytes.

Data & recovery · Level 2

Can we say exactly-once processing?
No. Transport is at-least-once. Inbox checks, idempotent transitions and worker fences aim at one correct logical result despite duplicates.

Data & recovery · Level 2

Why is a queue visibility timeout not enough to protect completion?
Visibility controls delivery, not ownership of job state; a worker can outlive its message’s invisibility. The SQL attempt and lease fence decides if it may complete.

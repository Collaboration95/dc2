generator-output: what the load generator writes

scripts/aws/v1/proof/generator.py prints DC2-SAMPLES <index> <total> <chunk> lines (zlib-compressed, base64-encoded CSV with columns route, phase, user, started_at, ended_at, elapsed_ms, status_code, error) and one DC2-RESULT {json} line (ok, samples_sha256, users, routes with count, errors, p50, p90, p95, p99 and max, windows, python, cpus). Defaults: 50 users, 180 s, 5 s warm-up, 20 s per other route, 20 logins. Decoding and verdict rules: collect_samples, parse_samples and judge in scripts/aws/v1/proof/latency.py (status p90 at most 700 ms, login p90 under 2 s, errors under 1%, Gateway throttle at least 50 requests/s with burst 100). Local-stack alternative: scripts/evidence/performance.py (writes requests.jsonl, queue.jsonl, targets.csv).
